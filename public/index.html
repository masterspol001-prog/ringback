FAQ

How numbers, data, emergencies, and unknowns work

How do phone numbers work?

You keep your existing business number. Unanswered calls forward to RingBack, which texts the caller back as your shop. We do not replace the number customers already know.

How is customer data handled?

We store what is needed to recover the job: name, phone, messages, qualification, appointment, and status. You can export or delete it. We do not sell lead data.

SMS compliance

Every outbound text includes a way to stop. STOP / opt-out marks the lead do-not-contact and cancels follow-up. We only text people who called or messaged your business.

Call recording and transcription

Missed-call recovery today is SMS after the unanswered call. Live AI answering is a later plan. If recording is ever enabled, it is opt-in and disclosed. Nothing is recorded by default in this product.

Security and retention

Access is session-authenticated. Data lives in your RingBack database (SQLite or JSON). Retention is until you export or delete. No fake trust badges.

Human escalation

You can take over any conversation. RingBack pauses auto-replies until you return it. Urgent leads also alert you instead of pretending to dispatch.

What happens during emergencies?

Gas smell, flooding, sparking, or similar language is Emergency detected. RingBack applies your shop policy, notifies the owner, and tells the customer what is actually known. Dispatcher confirmed and Technician dispatched are separate states. RingBack never claims a technician is on the way unless you confirm. If no emergency policy is configured, it fails safely.

What if RingBack does not know the answer?

It asks one more qualifying question or offers a booking slot. It never invents prices, availability, or policies. FAQs only use copy you approved.

Is there a contract?

No. Month to month. The first 10 customers get setup free. We do not guarantee ROI.
